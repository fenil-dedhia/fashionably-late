## Description  (full listing body)

```
Being fashionably late is a good thing — for parties, and for email too.

If you work across time zones and keep doing the mental math on when to hit send, Fashionably Late does it for you. It enhances Gmail's own Schedule Send — same familiar buttons and the native "Scheduled" label, just smarter about timing.

WHAT IT DOES

• The right local time for your recipient. When you schedule an email, Fashionably Late reads the recipients from your compose window, lets you pick the one whose timing matters most, and offers a morning or midday slot in THEIR local time zone — so your message lands during their working day instead of getting buried overnight. Pick someone's time zone once and it's remembered.

• A friendly time-zone picker. Search by city, country, abbreviation, or UTC offset, with a Pinned section so the zones you work across most often sit at the top.

• A working-hours guard that stays out of your way. Before a regular Send goes out past the working hours you set, you get three clear choices: send anyway, snap to your working hours, or cancel. Scheduling for later never warns — timing a message for a recipient's window is exactly what this is for.

• A full settings panel. Manage your own time zone, pinned zones, working hours, the locally cached recipient zones, and feature toggles.

• Accessible by design — built to WCAG AA with full keyboard support.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Fashionably Late runs entirely in your browser. There is no account to create and no inbox to connect.

• No Google sign-in and no Gmail API. It reads only the recipients in your compose window to suggest good send times — never your message content.
• Stored entirely on your device. No Fashionably Late server, no backend.
• No tracking, no telemetry, no analytics.
• You stay in control — edit, export, or delete everything from Settings at any time.

It enhances Gmail's native Schedule Send rather than replacing it: the "Schedule send" option becomes "Schedule send (powered by Fashionably Late)," the familiar dialog gains a few time-zone-aware options, and Gmail's own Scheduled label is preserved.

Open source (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/fenil-dedhia/fashionably-late
```
